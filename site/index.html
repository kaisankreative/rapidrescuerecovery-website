FAQs

Questions people ask us

How quickly can you get to me?

It depends on where you are, the time of day and the traffic. We'll give you an honest arrival time when you call, and keep you updated on WhatsApp so you're never left guessing.

How much does vehicle recovery cost?

Every job is different, so we give you a fixed price before we set off. The price depends on distance, time of day, the vehicle and how easy it is to load. No hidden extras, no surprises.

Do I need to be a member or have breakdown cover?

No. We're pay-as-you-go. Call or WhatsApp, agree the price, and that's it.

Do you cover outside Telford?

Yes. We cover Telford, Shropshire and the West Midlands with our own truck, and anywhere in the UK through our trusted partner network, with one point of contact throughout.

What vehicles can you recover?

Cars, vans up to 3.5 tonnes, 4x4s, SUVs, automatics, and electric and hybrid vehicles. Everything is carried fully on the bed of our truck, which is the safest way to move them.

Where can you take my vehicle?

Anywhere you need: your home, a local garage, a main dealer, a bodyshop, or a destination anywhere in the UK.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfer or cash. You'll get a clear invoice for every job.

Do you work with garages and car dealers?

Yes. We run regular collections and deliveries for garages, dealers and bodyshops. See trade accounts.
